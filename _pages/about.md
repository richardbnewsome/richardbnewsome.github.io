I am a Research Assistant for the Office of the Chief Economist: Middle East, North Africa, Afghanistan & Pakistan (MENAAP) at the World Bank. 

My research focuses on development economics, and in recent research, I have leveraged spatial data in data-sparse environments. Please refer to the Publications tab for examples, including *Fields of War: Conflict and Climate Vulnerability across the Syrian-Turkish Border* (Newsome et al., 2026) and *Locked Out: The Uneven Toll of COVID Border Closures in Sub-Saharan Africa* (Newsome, 2026).

I hold a Master's degree in Global Human Development from Georgetown University (2025), as well as a Bachelor's degree in Physics from the Georgia Institute of Technology (2019).
